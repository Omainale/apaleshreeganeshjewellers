Our Story

Three generations of master craftsmen, one unwavering commitment to excellence

A Legacy of Excellence

Founded in 1965 by Shri Ganesh Patel, Shree Ganesh Jewellers began as a small workshop in the heart of Mumbai's historic jewellery district. What started as one man's passion for creating beautiful adornments has grown into a family legacy spanning three generations.

Today, we continue to honor our founder's vision: to create jewellery that transcends time, combining traditional Indian craftsmanship with contemporary design sensibilities. Each piece that leaves our atelier carries with it decades of expertise, love, and attention to detail.

Our showroom welcomes clients from across the globe who seek not just jewellery, but treasured heirlooms that will be passed down through generations.

Master Craftsmanship

At Shree Ganesh Jewellers, we believe that true luxury lies in the details. Our master artisans, many of whom have been with us for over two decades, bring unparalleled skill to every creation.

From the initial sketch to the final polish, each piece undergoes meticulous scrutiny at every stage. We employ time-honored techniques such as jadau, kundan, and meenakari work, while also embracing modern precision technology to ensure perfection.

Our workshop is where art meets science, tradition meets innovation, and precious materials are transformed into wearable poetry.

Ethical Sourcing

We believe that true beauty comes from responsible practices. Every diamond, gemstone, and precious metal in our collection is ethically sourced and certified conflict-free.

We maintain direct relationships with trusted suppliers and mines, ensuring complete transparency in our supply chain. Our diamonds are certified by GIA and all our gold carries BIS hallmarking.

When you choose Shree Ganesh Jewellers, you're not just acquiring beautiful jewellery—you're supporting sustainable practices and fair trade throughout the industry.
